[ How it starts ]

From zero to a living workspace in five beats.

  1. 01

    Name the industry

    Tell AWXFORGE what you are. A mill, a clinic, a carrier, a campus, a grid.

  2. 02

    Take a blueprint

    Open a working model of that industry and step into a workspace with a shape.

  3. 03

    Connect the signals

    Bring in the tools you already run. The forge listens and draws one picture.

  4. 04

    Set the agents

    Point them at the repetitive work. They move it and show every step they took.

  5. 05

    Run from one place

    Teams come back to the same forge. The work has a home.
